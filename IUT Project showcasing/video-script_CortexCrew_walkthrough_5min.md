## Why this works (don't cut these)

- **Open loop → literal payoff:** Block 1 promises "the answer on one chart"; Block 6 shows it (better — −37.8%). Judges wait for it.
- **The honest time-skip** (Block 3) reads as professional, not as cheating — everyone who has demoed AI respects it.
- **Tech credibility** (Block 4): "latest GPT model + our medical system prompt + never diagnoses" is one sentence — enough for expert judges, not boring for the rest.
- **Every claim is visible on screen** — history-aware summary, red ranges, doctor questions, auto-filing, the improvement insight. Say it while pointing at it.

## Recording tips

- One block per take is fine — blocks cut cleanly at screen changes.
- Speak to one friend, not "the judges." Small stumbles are human — keep going.
- If a block runs long, cut words, never talk faster.
- Dry-run once with a stopwatch. Total must be under 5:00 — you have 10 s spare.
