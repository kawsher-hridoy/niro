Submission Instructions:

Submit your abstract in PDF format only (maximum 500 words).
Abstract File Naming Format: ProjectShowcasing_Techathon_TeamName_FirstRound.pdf
Submit a 3–5 minute project demonstration video.
Video File Naming Format: ProjectShowcasing_Techathon_TeamName_FirstRound.mp4
Upload the video to your personal Google Drive, set the sharing permission to "Anyone with the link can view," and submit the Google Drive link through the Google Form.
Only one submission per team is required. The Team Leader should submit both the abstract and the video link.
Late submissions will not be accepted.
Submissions that are corrupted, incorrectly formatted, inaccessible, or do not follow the submission guidelines may be disqualified.
After evaluation, the top 10 teams from each category will qualify for the Final Round (On-Campus).
