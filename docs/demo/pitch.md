# Demo — Verbal Pitch

The 5 talking points that anchor every pitch, whether 30 seconds or 5
minutes. Memorize these. Everything else is improvisation.

## The pitch line

> **"Niro is Bangladesh's first patient-owned medical record. AI explains
> every prescription and report in Bangla, and gives every doctor — or
> you — your full medical picture in 30 seconds."**

If you forget everything else, say this.

## The 5 talking points

### 1. The problem is universal

Every Bangladeshi has:
- A handwritten prescription they can't read.
- A lab report they don't understand.
- Visited multiple doctors who don't know each other's prescriptions.
- Paid 800–2000 taka for an in-person second opinion when they had a
  five-minute question.

### 2. Three connected solutions

Niro is one app that does three things, in order of effort:

1. **AI Analyzer:** photo → Bangla explanation in 3 seconds.
2. **Patient Profile:** every analyzed document becomes a timeline
   entry. The AI uses your full history for the next analysis.
3. **On-Demand Doctor Verification + Chamber Sharing:** real doctor
   verifies the AI's read for 200-800 BDT; or you walk into any chamber
   and the doctor sees your full history in 30 seconds.

### 3. The genuine innovation

> **The AI prepares the case for the doctor.**

That's why a doctor's verification can be cheap: they don't spend 30
minutes reading; they spend 5 minutes confirming. AI does the
preparatory work; humans do the judgment work. Everyone wins.

### 4. The differentiator

| Bangladesh competitor | Does | Doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| Praava Health | Telemedicine + clinics + EMR | Closed to Praava network only |
| Daktarbhai | Consult + directory | No AI, unreliable reviews |
| Tonic (GP) | Phone telemedicine | No documents, no profile |
| Arogga / MedEasy | Prescription → pharmacy delivery | No explanation, no second opinion |

**Nobody combines:** patient-owned EMR + AI analysis + offline chamber
sharing + verified-consult-only reviews.

### 5. The trust architecture

> **"AI never gives final medical advice. Only extracts, explains, and
> flags."**

- Every AI output carries a visible disclaimer.
- Confidence scores; low confidence auto-suggests human review.
- Banned-phrase linter blocks imperative dosing language.
- Every AI call logged in an append-only audit trail.
- Every doctor view of patient data requires explicit, time-bound consent.
- Patient sees every access; can revoke one-tap.
- Built for DPA 2023 from day one — patient owns and can delete all data.

This is the answer to every skeptical question.

## Variants by audience

### To a clinical judge

Lead with the **AI safety architecture** (point 5) and the **AI-prepared
case** angle (point 3). Show the access log.

### To a technical judge

Lead with **the differentiator** (point 4) and the **AI provider
abstraction** + **chamber QR flow**. Show the architecture diagram.

### To a business judge

Lead with **the problem is universal** (point 1) and the **business
model** (subscription + tiered review + free doctor onboarding =
network effect). Show the directory.

### To a community / impact judge

Lead with **rural access** and **Bangla-first** angle. Show the lab
report explanation in Bangla. Frame chamber QR as "works in every
chamber in Bangladesh, online or offline."

## What to never say

- "It's like ChatGPT for health." (Reduces the architecture story.)
- "We're disrupting healthcare." (Cringe; specific is better.)
- "The AI diagnoses." (We never diagnose. Hard line.)
- "It's HIPAA compliant." (We're in Bangladesh; the regulatory frame is
  DPA 2023, not HIPAA. Mixing them sounds careless.)

## Three numbers to remember

- **৫ মিনিট / 5 minutes:** how long a doctor's review takes when AI
  prepares the case. (Was 30 minutes.)
- **৪০০ টাকা / 400 BDT:** mid-tier verification fee. (Was 1500-2000 in
  person.)
- **৩০ সেকেন্ড / 30 seconds:** time for a chamber doctor to read your
  full history when you scan the QR.

## Closing line

> **"Niro doesn't replace doctors. It makes every doctor 5× faster, and
> every patient 5× more informed. Ready for pilot in any Dhaka chamber.
> Thank you."**
