## PART 1 — HOOK  ·  *0:00–0:20*

> *(No screen yet. Look at the judges. Hold up a real handwritten prescription.)*

"Raise your hand if you can read this."

*(beat — nobody can)*

"Neither can the patient it was given to. In Bangladesh, a handwritten chit like this is the **only** medical record 170 million people have — and most can't read a word of it. Not the drug, not the dose, not the warning."

> **Delivery:** Slow. Let the silence after "Raise your hand" do the work. This is the emotional anchor — make them *feel* the problem before you name it.

---

## PART 2 — PROBLEM & WHO  ·  *0:20–0:50*

"It's not just the prescription. The lab report is a wall of numbers with no explanation, so people Google their results and panic. A real second opinion costs **eight hundred to two thousand taka**, plus travel, plus half a day off work — so most people never get one. And every doctor visit starts from zero, because there's no portable history. The doctor spends ten of fifteen minutes just asking *'what are you taking?'* instead of treating you.

This hits **everyone** — the rickshaw puller and the professor alike — but hardest the people who can least afford to be confused about their own health."

> **Delivery:** Brisk, factual, stacking. Each pain point is one breath. The numbers (800–2000 Tk, 10-of-15 minutes) are your credibility — say them crisply.

---

## PART 3 — SOLUTION + DEMO  ·  *0:50–2:10*  ·  **LIVE — let the product talk**

"This is **Niro** — Bangladesh's first patient-owned medical record. Let me show you, not tell you."

> *(Switch to screen. You're already on **Rahima's** profile.)*

**[0:55] Profile** — "Rahima is a real patient profile we seeded. Here's her timeline — past prescriptions, reports, every AI reading, all in one place."

**[1:05] Upload** — "Now she gets a new prescription." *(Photograph the real prescription → upload as a prescription.)* "Watch."

**[1:15] AI reads it — in Bangla** — *(Analysis appears in under 8 seconds.)* "In seconds, Niro extracts every medication, the dose, the duration — and explains it in **plain Bangla**, with Bangla numerals. No jargon."

**[1:35] The flag — this is the moment** — "And here's what no other app does. Niro knows Rahima's history. She's been on **Metformin** since January. This new prescription adds **Glimepiride** — and Niro flags the interaction automatically. The AI doesn't just read one page. It reads her *whole story*."

**[1:55] Human verification** — "Not sure? One tap — *Request Verification*. A real BMDC-licensed doctor reviews the AI's read and the original document, for as little as **200 taka**, same day. The patient stays in control of exactly what's shared, and for how long."

> **Delivery:** This is the heart. Slow down at the **flag** (1:35) — pause, point at the screen, let it land. If the live upload is risky, pre-load the analysis and *re-run* it; have the video fallback ready.

---

## PART 4 — IMPACT & TECH  ·  *2:10–2:45*

"Why does this scale? Because the AI prepares a one-page case summary, a doctor reviews in **5 minutes instead of 30** — *that's* why the second opinion can be cheap. And it works where **90% of Bangladeshi care actually happens**: the offline chamber. Patient scans a QR on the doctor's tablet, shares her full history for the length of the visit, and it auto-revokes when she leaves.

Under the hood: a **multimodal LLM** reading photos and PDFs, **structured extraction**, a **confidence score on every claim** — and when confidence is low, Niro automatically recommends a human. The AI **never** gives final medical advice. It explains, it flags, and it gets out of the way. Every decision is logged, every access is consented and auditable. It's **live right now at nirobd.tech**."

> **Delivery:** Confident, fast. This is the "we actually built it and it's safe" section. Hit *5-minutes-instead-of-30*, *90% of care*, and *never gives final advice* — those three are your defensibility.

---

## PART 5 — VISION & ASK  ·  *2:45–3:00*

"Bangladesh has talked about a patient-owned health record for a decade and never built it. We did — and it ships in Bangla, for the offline chamber, today.

We don't replace doctors. We hand them the patient in 30 seconds.

**Niro — আপনার স্বাস্থ্য, আপন হাতে। Your health, in your own hands.**

We're ready to pilot in any chamber in Dhaka. Thank you."

> **Delivery:** Land the tagline in Bangla, then English. Eye contact, stop talking, smile. Don't trail off — the last line is the one they'll remember.
