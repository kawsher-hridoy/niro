# Niro — "A Son's Story" (Story-Led Emotional Cut)
### Infinity AI BuildFest 2026 · Track 3 (HealthTech) · 180s Video Script — v1

**Format:** Emotional founder story (b-roll + voice) for 0:00–1:10, then live app screen-recording for the solution.
**Voiceover:** **Bangla** (first person, from the heart) with **burned-in English subtitles**. The mother tongue carries the emotion; subtitles open it to a global BuildFest audience.
**Target spoken length:** ~340 Bangla words (paced slow, ~115 wpm, with pauses for silence).
**Tone:** quiet, honest, personal → then confident and capable. Earn the emotion, then prove the product.
**Golden rule:** Don't tell the judge to feel. Show one true detail, hold the silence, let them feel it.

---

## SEGMENT 1 — THE STORY (0:00–0:55) · "Feel the problem"

**ON SCREEN (shootable on a phone, no budget needed):**
- A pile of real (redacted) prescriptions + a plastic bag of crumpled lab reports on a table.
- Close-ups: a strip of pills, a glucometer, a blood-pressure cuff.
- A hand picking up one prescription, turning it — unreadable handwriting.
- Night: a phone glowing in the dark, a report photo open in ChatGPT.
- (Optional) founder talking-head, soft side light, no smile.

**VOICEOVER (Bangla) — [English subtitle]:**

> "এই গল্পটা আমার। হয়তো আপনারও।"
> *[This story is mine. Maybe yours too.]*
>
> "কিছুদিন আগে আব্বুর হার্টে সমস্যা ধরা পড়ল। আগে থেকেই ছিল ডায়াবেটিস।"
> *[A while ago my father was diagnosed with a heart condition. He already had diabetes.]*
>
> "তারপর থেকে আমাদের জীবন মানেই — ডাক্তার, টেস্ট, আবার ডাক্তার।"
> *[After that, our life became — doctors, tests, doctors again.]*
>
> "প্রতিবার বাসায় ফিরতাম একগাদা রিপোর্ট আর প্রেসক্রিপশন হাতে নিয়ে।"
> *[Each time we came home with a fistful of reports and prescriptions.]*
>
> "আমি মেডিকেলের লোক না। হাতের কাগজটার একটা শব্দও বুঝতাম না।"
> *[I'm not a medical person. I couldn't understand a single word on the page in my hand.]*
>
> "ডায়াবেটিস, তার ওপর হার্ট — একসাথে সাত-আটটা ওষুধ। কোনটা কোনটার সাথে চলবে না, জানতাম না।"
> *[Diabetes, and now the heart — seven, eight medicines at once. Which one clashes with which, I had no idea.]*

> *[Inside, one fear haunted me — what if one mistake of mine harms my father?]*

## Production notes
- [ ] **Authenticity beats polish.** Record the Bangla VO in your own voice, even if it shakes. A real tremor on *"আমার একটা ভুলে..."* is worth more than any studio take.
- [ ] **Use redacted real reports** (tape over the name/ID) for Segment 1 — real handwriting sells the pain. Use the synthetic **"Rahima" 10-report set** (`test-reports/`) for the live trend-chart demo so the curve looks real.
- [ ] Two silences are load-bearing: after *"কিছু হয়ে যায়?"* (0:50) and after *"ভয়টা কমিয়ে দিয়েছে"* (2:53). Don't fill them.
- [ ] Shoot Segment 1 b-roll on a phone at night, warm/dim. Shoot the demo bright and crisp. The visual shift from dark→light mirrors the emotional arc.
- [ ] Burn in English subtitles (judges may not enable captions). Keep them one line, centered-bottom.
- [ ] App in **Bangla**, font ≥18px, browser extensions off (avoid hydration noise).

## 90-second ICADHI cut (if you need it shorter)
Keep Segments 1, 3, 6 intact (the story spine). Compress Segment 4 to two beats — the Bangla analysis + the diabetes/heart interaction warning. Drop Segment 5's second line. The emotional arc survives; only demo breadth is trimmed.

## Strategic note (honest)
The emotional open earns attention — but BuildFest/IEEE judges score **feasibility, depth, and real AI**. Segment 4 must visibly show a *working deployed* product (it is — nirobd.tech), and Segment 5's "never prescribes / audit-logged" is your responsible-AI proof. Lead with the heart; close with the engineering. Don't let the demo feel like a slideshow — show the live app responding.
